GREEN MINDS

Project Name:
Green Minds

Team Name:
Agri Innovator

Project Tagline:
AI-powered agricultural assistant for Indian farmers

Problem:
Farmers may find it difficult to get simple and useful farming guidance based on their crop, soil, weather, and location.

Solution:
Green Minds is an AI-powered agricultural assistant that provides simple farming guidance using the information entered by the farmer.

Key Features:
1. AI-powered farming guidance
2. Crop and soil-based advice
3. Weather-based suggestions
4. Location-based farming support
5. Simple and farmer-friendly interface

How It Works:
1. The farmer enters their details.
2. The farmer enters crop, soil, weather, and location information.
3. The farmer clicks the Get AI Advice button.
4. Green Minds sends the information to the AI service.
5. The AI generates farming guidance.
6. The advice is displayed on the website.

Technology Used:
- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- Python
- Flask
- Gemini AI

Project Goal:
To provide accessible and easy-to-understand AI-powered farming guidance for Indian farmers.
