GREEN MINDS – HACKATHON DEMO SCRIPT

Hello everyone.

Our project is called Green Minds.
Our team name is Agri Innovator.

Green Minds is an AI-powered agricultural assistant for Indian farmers.

The main idea is to provide simple farming guidance based on the farmer's crop, soil, weather, and location details.

First, I enter the farmer's details.
Then I select the crop, soil, and weather information.

Now I click the Get AI Advice button.

Green Minds sends the information to the AI service.
The AI processes the information and generates practical farming guidance.

The advice is then displayed on the website.

Our project uses HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Flask, and Gemini AI.

The goal of Green Minds is to make AI-powered farming guidance simple and accessible for Indian farmers.

Thank you.
