About Green Minds

Green Minds is an AI-powered agricultural assistant designed to help Indian farmers make better farming decisions. It provides simple and practical guidance using crop, soil, weather, and location details.

Key Features

  • AI-powered farming guidance
  • Advice based on crop and soil
  • Weather-based suggestions
  • Location-based farming support
  • Simple and farmer-friendly interface

How It Works

  1. Enter farmer and farming details.
  2. Click the Get AI Advice button.
  3. Green Minds sends the details to the AI service.
  4. The AI generates practical farming guidance.
  5. The advice is displayed on the website.
